TikTok Backstage

Not connected

This dashboard does not access your TikTok account, impersonate Backstage, or install plugins inside it.

  1. Confirm the integration permissions available to your agency with TikTok.
  2. Obtain official documentation and approval for your markets.
  3. Implement and verify the approved integration before enabling sync.

Your handle is a workspace label, not proof of account ownership or an account link.

↗

Lark

Not configured

Optional server-side connection using Lark's official app and messaging APIs. No community plugins.

  1. Create an internal app in your Lark developer console.
  2. Enable bot messaging permissions, publish the app, and add the bot to the intended chat.
  3. Configure the app ID, app secret, and chat ID on your server, then restart it.

Configured does not mean verified. This sends one message; it does not sync creators or calendars.
